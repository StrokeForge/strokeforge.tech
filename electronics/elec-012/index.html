Selection guide

Fast (F) vs Slow-blow (T)

  • Fast (F) — blows quickly on any overcurrent. Use for sensitive electronics, semiconductors, PCB protection
  • Slow (T) — tolerates short inrush surges. Use for motors, transformers, switching supplies
  • Medium (M) — compromise. General purpose, lamps, heaters
  • When in doubt with motors: choose T (slow-blow)

Rating rule of thumb

  • Fuse rating = 125–150 % of max operating current
  • Always round up to the next standard value
  • Motor inrush can be 6–10× normal current for 50–200 ms
  • Switching PSU inrush: 3–5× for a few milliseconds
  • Fuse must blow before any component is damaged

Voltage rating

  • Fuse voltage rating must be ≥ supply voltage
  • Do NOT use a 32 V automotive fuse on 230 V AC
  • DC fuses are different from AC fuses — arc quenching differs
  • Glass fuses: typically rated 250 V AC
  • Blade fuses: typically 32 V DC (automotive)
