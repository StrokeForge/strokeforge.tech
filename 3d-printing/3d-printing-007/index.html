Label

📁 3D Printing ⭐ Beginner

Project Description

These simple filament labels were designed to solve a common organization problem: identifying filaments stored in dryboxes, bins, and various containers. Generic labels like "PET Blue" aren't helpful when you have multiple blue PET filaments with different properties or manufacturers.

The solution is elegantly simple: print each label in the same color as the filament it identifies. Material cost? Essentially zero—just 7 grams per label. At a glance, you'll know exactly which filament is in each container. The label can include manufacturer, color code, purchase date, or any other details you find useful.

Build Process

Print the Labels

Print settings:

Material: PLA, PETG, ASA, or whatever matches the filament you're labeling
Perimeters: 2 walls
Infill: 100%
Layer height: 0.2mm (or adjust as preferred)
Supports: None required
Print time: ~30 minutes per label
Filament usage: ~2m (~7g) per label

Pro tip: Print each label in the color of the filament it will identify. The minimal material cost (7g) is worth the instant visual recognition.

Attach to Containers

Use double-sided tape to attach labels to your dryboxes, storage bins, or filament bags. The labels are designed to be visible and easy to read at a glance.

Write or print information on the label surface using a permanent marker, label maker, or printed paper insert (depending on your label design).

Learned Lessons

💡 Simplicity is Beauty

Sometimes the most effective solutions are the simplest ones. A small printed label in matching filament color solves a real organizational problem with minimal material cost and zero complexity. When you can solve a problem with 7 grams of plastic and 30 minutes of print time, that's good design.

Problems & Solutions

✅ No Problems Encountered

This is a straightforward print with no special requirements. The simple geometry and solid infill mean there's nothing that can go wrong—just slice, print, and attach with double-sided tape.
