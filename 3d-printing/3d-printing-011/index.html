Chesse Game

📁 3D Printing ⭐ Intermediate ⏱️ ~38 hours total

Project Description

A strategy board game where players use rubber bands to claim territory on a pin board. My parents sent me a photo of this game wanting one for my niece, but it was unavailable at the time — so I had to improvise and design it from scratch.

The game develops spatial awareness, strategic planning, opponent blocking, and predicting future moves. Suitable for 2 players (optionally 3–4 with extra sets).

How to Play

Setup

Place the board between players. Each player chooses a color (rubber bands and markers). Keep markers in front of you. Decide who goes first (e.g. by drawing lots).

Gameplay

Players take turns. On each turn, take one rubber band and stretch it around at least 3 pins to create a closed geometric shape. The band must be taut around the pins only — no loose throws. Once placed, the band cannot be moved or removed.

Claiming Territory

You claim all free areas completely inside your newly created shape that aren't already marked by the opponent. Mark claimed fields immediately with your markers.

Rules & Restrictions

Bands may touch other bands but cannot displace them. You cannot move opponent's bands or claim already marked fields. The shape must be fully closed with no gaps.

End & Scoring

The game ends when no new closed shapes can be formed or players run out of rubber bands. Each player counts their marked fields — the player with more fields wins. Tie = draw.

Optional Variants

Variant A: Shape must contain at least one new field, otherwise the move is invalid.
Variant B: Micro-triangles containing less than one full field are not allowed.
Variant C: Claiming 3+ fields in a single turn grants a bonus turn.

Problems & Solutions

⚠️ Game Board Warping

The large game board had significant warping issues during printing due to its size.

✅ Solution

Added a brim and used adhesive (glue stick) on the print bed. This kept the board flat throughout the long 20+ hour print.
