Formulas — EN 1993-1-8 §3.6

Bolt shear resistance

F_v,Rd = αv · fub · As / γM2
αv = 0.6 (grade 4.6–6.8, shank)
αv = 0.5 (grade 4.6–6.8, thread)
αv = 0.6 (grade 8.8–12.9)

This tool assumes shear through the threaded portion (αv = 0.5 for 4.6, 0.6 for 8.8+). γM2 = 1.25.

Bearing resistance

F_b,Rd = k₁ · αb · fu · d · t / γM2
αb = min(e₁/3d₀, p₁/3d₀−¼, fub/fu, 1)
k₁ = min(2.8·e₂/d₀−1.7, 2.5)

e₂/d₀ assumed = 1.5 (edge distance perp. to load). Governing failure mode is the minimum of shear and bearing.

Group resistance

F_Rd = n · min(F_v,Rd, F_b,Rd)
η = F_Ed / F_Rd ≤ 1.0

Assumes equal load distribution (rigid group, identical bolts in line).

Partial factors

  • γM2 = 1.25 (bolts, welds)
  • γM0 = 1.00 (cross-sections)
  • Preloaded bolts (slip-critical) are not checked here
  • Standard: ČSN EN 1993-1-8:2006, Table 3.4
