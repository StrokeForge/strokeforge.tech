Formulas — ISO 281

Basic rating life

L10 = (C/P)^p  [10⁶ rev]
p = 3     (ball bearings)
p = 10/3  (roller bearings)
L10h = L10·10⁶ / (60·n)

Reliability-adjusted life

L10m = a1 · L10  [10⁶ rev]
L10mh = a1 · L10h  [hours]
