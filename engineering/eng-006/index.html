Method — EC3 Simplified

Design resistance

Per ČSN EN 1993-1-8 §4.5.3.3 (simplified method):

fw,Rd = fu / (√3 · βw · γM2)

Correlation factor βw: S235→0.80, S275→0.85, S355→0.90, S460→1.00

Stress calculation

  • Tension/Compression: σ = F / Aw
  • Shear: τ = V / Aw
  • Bending: σ = M / (a · Ww) where Ww = n·l²/6

Aw = a × leff × n [mm²]

Weld geometry rules (EC3)

  • Min. throat: a ≥ 3 mm (structural)
  • Max. throat: a ≤ 0.7 × tmin
  • Min. effective length: leff ≥ 6a and ≥ 30 mm
  • Start/stop deduction: 2a per weld

Material data used

  • S235: fu = 360 MPa, βw = 0.80
  • S275: fu = 430 MPa, βw = 0.85
  • S355: fu = 490 MPa, βw = 0.90
  • S460: fu = 570 MPa, βw = 1.00
Weld throat vs. leg size

Throat (a) vs. leg (z)

For a 90° fillet weld between two plates:

a = z / √2 ≈ 0.707 × z

A weld with 6 mm legs has a throat of ≈ 4.2 mm. Always design using throat a, not leg size z.

Weld quality classes (EN ISO 5817)

  • B — highest quality, full penetration, fatigue critical
  • C — normal structural welds (most common)
  • D — lower quality, non-structural

This calculator assumes quality C or better — verify inspection requirements for your application.
