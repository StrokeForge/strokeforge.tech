Column definitions

Dimensions

  • h — total height [mm]
  • b — flange width [mm]
  • tw — web thickness [mm]
  • tf — flange thickness [mm]
  • r — root radius [mm] (I/U sections)

Section properties

  • A — cross-sectional area [cm²]
  • Ix — moment of inertia, strong axis [cm⁴]
  • Iy — moment of inertia, weak axis [cm⁴]
  • Wx — elastic section modulus, strong axis [cm³]
  • Wy — elastic section modulus, weak axis [cm³]
  • ix — radius of gyration, strong axis [cm]
  • iy — radius of gyration, weak axis [cm]

Standards

  • IPE — EN 10034
  • HEA / HEB — EN 10034
  • UPN — EN 10279
  • L equal leg — EN 10056-1

Notes

Values are nominal (elastic) section properties. Plastic section moduli (Wpl) are not listed here — use the Cross-Section Properties tool for computed values. For design use, always verify against official mill certificates or EN tables.
