Formulas

UDL — uniform distributed load

R_A = q·L
M_A = q·L²/2  (at fixed end)
f_tip = q·L⁴/(8·E·Ix)

Point load F at free end

R_A = F
M_A = F·L  (at fixed end)
f_tip = F·L³/(3·E·Ix)

Sign convention

Fixed end A is on the left. Reaction R_A acts upward. Moment M_A is the restraining moment at the wall (hogging). Deflection f_tip is downward at the free tip.

Serviceability (L/δ)

  • General: L/150 – L/200
  • Floors: L/300 – L/360
  • Brittle finishes: L/360
  • Cantilevers: typically L/180
