How to use

1. Enter the nominal diameter in mm (0–500 mm range per ISO 286-1).
2. Select hole tolerance (e.g. H7) and shaft tolerance (e.g. f7, k6, p6).
3. Click Calculate to see limit deviations, fit type, and tolerance zone diagram.

Sign convention: Positive deviation = above nominal line. Negative = below.
Hole: ES = upper deviation, EI = lower deviation.
Shaft: es = upper deviation, ei = lower deviation.

IT grades available: IT01, IT0, IT1 … IT18.
Fundamental deviations for holes: A–ZC. For shafts: a–zc.
