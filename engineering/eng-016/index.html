Method — EN 1993-1-8 directional method

Stresses on weld throat

σ⊥ = τ⊥ = (N/Aw + M/Ww) / √2
τ‖ = V / Aw
Aw = a · l_eff · n
Ww = n · a · l_eff² / 6

A transverse force on the 45° throat splits equally into σ⊥ and τ⊥ (the √2 factor); the shear V acts as τ‖.

Resistance check (EN 1993-1-8 §4.5.3.2)

[σ⊥² + 3(τ⊥² + τ‖²)]½ ≤ fu/(βw·γM2)
σ⊥ ≤ 0.9·fu/γM2

γM2 = 1.25; βw = 0.80 (S235) … 1.00 (S420/S460). leff = l − 2a deducts the end craters.
